## 🔍 What I actually do

I stare at logs until something looks wrong. Then I pull the thread until the whole story falls out.

## 🚩 Stuff that makes me look twice

- PowerShell that's hiding what it's doing
- Word spawning a shell (nice try)
- `svchost.exe` running from the wrong folder
- Anything poking at LSASS
- A sudden burst of `whoami`, `hostname` and `net`
- Logins at weird times from weird places
- That one process calling out when it really shouldn't

## 🧠 How I hunt
